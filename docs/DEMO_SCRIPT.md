# Demo script

Duration: about six minutes. No manual database changes at any point.

1. **Overview** — recent analyses and counts from the live database. Click _Start New Analysis_.
2. **Load a demo scenario** — choose _Ashwagandha water-soluble extract_. All five steps prefill; point out that every field is editable.
3. **Step through** Product, Ingredients (Withania somnifera, cultivated, Madhya Pradesh), Claims (wellness wording), Preparation / Innovation (novel aqueous extraction), Review. Click _Run Analysis_.
4. **Progress** — the pipeline stages are real steps: normalisation, classification, retrieval, validation, explanation, routing, checklist.
5. **Result** — four-dimension classification with confidence, overall confidence label, and the missing-information list. Emphasise: preliminary assessment, not a legal determination.
6. **Evidence** — open an evidence card: authority, section, jurisdiction, version, effective date, why it was retrieved, and _Open Official Source_. Open the drawer to show evidence separated from system interpretation.
7. **Routing** — IP pathways worded as _potentially relevant_, regulatory routes with the potentially applicable authority, TK screening (no TKDL access claimed), ABS screening, market considerations.
8. **Checklist** — prioritised actions with reason, evidence reference, owner and status. Change one status.
9. **Expert review** — show the escalation with its reason and the packet contents; note the calm amber treatment, not an error.
10. **Language** — switch to हिन्दी; the interface and the saved narrative appear in Hindi while source titles, sections and scientific names stay unchanged.
11. **Cases** — reopen the saved case and show the audit history of the analysis.

## Other scenarios

_Classical Ayurvedic formulation_ (classical pathway, no innovation), _Ayurveda Aahara food product_ (FSSAI route), _Brahmi phytosome_ (novel process, patent and prior-art focus), _Herbal Immunity Syrup_ (mixed claims, wild-collected resource, expert review required).

## What not to claim

No TKDL search, no accuracy percentage that was not measured, no legal approval, no guarantee of patentability.
